Jest 30 Removed toThrowError and Other Aliases

The page recommended .toThrowError(ErrorClass), and the cheat sheet used it too. In Jest 30 that alias is gone, along with toBeCalled, toBeCalledWith, lastCalledWith and the toReturn family.
