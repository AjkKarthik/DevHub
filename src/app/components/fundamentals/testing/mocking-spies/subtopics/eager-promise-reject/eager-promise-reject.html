mockReturnValue(Promise.reject()) Rejects Too Early

The quiz called mockResolvedValue a shorthand for mockReturnValue(Promise.resolve(v)). That is close for resolved values, but for rejections the two behave differently, and the difference shows up as an unhandled rejection.
