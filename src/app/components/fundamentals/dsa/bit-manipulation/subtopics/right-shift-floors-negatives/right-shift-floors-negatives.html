Right Shift Floors Negatives, So It Is Not Plain Division

In JavaScript, -5 >> 1 is -3, and 3e9 >> 1 is negative. Shifts round down and work on 32-bit integers, which the "multiply and divide by 2^k" shorthand leaves out.
