Replacing an OwnsMany Collection Deletes and Reinserts Everything

The main page warns owned entities have no identity outside their owner. The unstated consequence: replacing the whole collection property (rather than mutating it in place) breaks reference identity entirely — every row gets deleted and reinserted, even unchanged ones.
