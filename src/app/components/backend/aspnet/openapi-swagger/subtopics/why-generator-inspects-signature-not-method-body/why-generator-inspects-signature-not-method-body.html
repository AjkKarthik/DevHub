Why the Generator Inspects the Signature, Not the Method Body

Calling TypedResults.Ok() and TypedResults.NotFound() inside a method does nothing for the spec if the method itself is still declared as returning bare Task<IResult>. The generator only reflects on the signature — it never looks inside.
