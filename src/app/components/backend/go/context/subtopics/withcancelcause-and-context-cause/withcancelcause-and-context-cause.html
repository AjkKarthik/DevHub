WithCancelCause and context.Cause()

ctx.Err() only ever returns one of two generic sentinels — context.WithCancelCause (Go 1.21+) lets every layer attach its own specific reason, retrievable via context.Cause() anywhere downstream, even from a parent's own cancellation.
