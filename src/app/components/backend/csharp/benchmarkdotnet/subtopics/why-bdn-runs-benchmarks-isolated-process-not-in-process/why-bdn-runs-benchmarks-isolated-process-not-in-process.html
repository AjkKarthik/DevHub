Why BenchmarkDotNet Runs Each Benchmark in Its Own Isolated Process, Not In-Process Inside BenchmarkRunner.Run

Every example on the main topic page reads as if BenchmarkRunner.Run calls your [Benchmark] methods directly. It doesn't — BDN generates, compiles, and launches a separate child process per job configuration, eliminating cross-benchmark JIT and GC contamination by construction.
