Testing That BigInt Zero (0n) Is Falsy and Skipped by Truthiness Narrowing

The main page's list of falsy values — null, undefined, 0, "", false, NaN — omits one: 0n, the BigInt zero, which is just as easy to accidentally treat as "absent" when it's a genuine value.
