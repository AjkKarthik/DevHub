em Compounds in Nested Elements, rem Does Not

A parent and child both declaring font-size: 1.25em compute to 20px and 25px — the exact same rem value stays identical at every nesting depth.

Compare nested em vs. nested rem font-sizes side by side — one genuinely compounds with each nesting level, the other never does.
