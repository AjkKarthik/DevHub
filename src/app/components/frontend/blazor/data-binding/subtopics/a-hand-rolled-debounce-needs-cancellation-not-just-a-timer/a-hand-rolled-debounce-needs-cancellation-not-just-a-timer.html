A Hand-Rolled Debounce Needs Cancellation, Not Just a Timer

Wrapping an expensive call in Task.Delay alone only shifts WHEN each call fires — it doesn't reduce how many times it fires. Genuine debouncing requires cancelling the previous pending delay every time a new one starts.
