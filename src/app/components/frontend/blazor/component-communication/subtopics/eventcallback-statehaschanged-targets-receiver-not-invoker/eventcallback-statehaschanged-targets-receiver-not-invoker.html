EventCallback's Auto-StateHasChanged Targets the Receiver, Not the Invoker

When a child calls InvokeAsync(), Blazor automatically re-renders whichever component OWNS the handler — typically the parent — never the child that made the call, and never every intermediate component in a multi-level forwarding chain.
