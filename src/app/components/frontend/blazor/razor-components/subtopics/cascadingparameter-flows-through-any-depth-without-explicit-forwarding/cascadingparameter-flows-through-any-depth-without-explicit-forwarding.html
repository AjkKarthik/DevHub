CascadingParameter Flows Through Any Depth Without Explicit Forwarding

A regular [Parameter] only reaches one level down and must be explicitly re-forwarded by every intermediate component — CascadingValue bypasses intermediate components entirely, reaching any descendant regardless of depth.
