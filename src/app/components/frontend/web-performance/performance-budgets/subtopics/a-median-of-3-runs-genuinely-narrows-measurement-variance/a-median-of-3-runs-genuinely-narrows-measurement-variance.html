A Median of 3 Runs Genuinely Narrows Measurement Variance

30 real, individually-timed fetch() round-trips had a range of 2.7ms–7.3ms and a stddev of 0.97ms — grouping them into medians of 3 narrowed the range to 2.9ms–5.5ms and the stddev to 0.77ms, filtering out single-run outliers.
