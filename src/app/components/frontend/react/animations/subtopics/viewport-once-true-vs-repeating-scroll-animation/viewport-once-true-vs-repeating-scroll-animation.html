Testing That viewport.once True Stops the whileInView Animation From Repeating on Every Scroll Re-entry

The theory says a scroll animation "repeats on every re-entry" without once — this scrolls two real boxes in and out of view repeatedly to confirm the default is genuinely a repeat, not a one-time play.

See it run

A tall scrollable page with two whileInView boxes — one with viewport={once: true}, one without it. Scroll them in and out repeatedly.
