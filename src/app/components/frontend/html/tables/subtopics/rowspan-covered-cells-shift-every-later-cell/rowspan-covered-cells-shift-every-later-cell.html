A Leftover rowspan Cell Shifts Every Cell After It

Forgetting to remove the cell a rowspan already covers doesn't drop that cell — it renders one column over, shifting everything after it. cellIndex can't even detect it.

Two identical-looking tables — one with the covered cell correctly removed, one with it mistakenly left in. Compare cellIndex (blind to the problem) against a true grid-column audit (which reveals it).
