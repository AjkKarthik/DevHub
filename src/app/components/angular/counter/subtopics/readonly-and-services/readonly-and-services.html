Signals in Services — Sharing State Across Components

A signal on a component only that component can see. Move it to an injectable service and every component in the app can share the exact same state — no @Input/@Output plumbing required.

See it run

A CartService holds a private writable signal, exposes it as readonly, and derives a total with computed() — all shared through one injected singleton.

Where this fits

You now have the full pattern for shared, encapsulated state: signal() and computed() living on a service, exposed as readonly, mutated only through methods you control. Last stop: how signals connect to the RxJS Observables you'll still meet in HTTP calls and route params, via toSignal() and toObservable().
