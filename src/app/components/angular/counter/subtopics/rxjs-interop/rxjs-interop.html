RxJS Interop — toSignal() and toObservable()

HttpClient, route params, and form valueChanges still hand you Observables. toSignal() and toObservable() are the bridge that lets your signal-based components talk to them without giving up either model.

See it run

A search-as-you-type box: the input's value lives in a plain signal(), toObservable() feeds it into debounceTime + distinctUntilChanged + switchMap, and toSignal() brings the results straight back into a signal the template reads directly.

You've completed Signals & Reactive State

signal() holds state, computed() derives it, effect() reacts to it, @if/@for render it, services share it, and toSignal()/toObservable() connect it to RxJS when you need to. That's the complete picture — every other Angular topic that uses signals is built from these same six pieces.
