ngOnChanges & ngOnInit

Signal inputs do not trigger ngOnChanges at all — the single biggest surprise when migrating from @Input() to input(), and effect() is the reactive replacement.

See it run

A child with BOTH a decorator input and a signal input — watch the console to see ngOnChanges fire for one and completely ignore the other.
