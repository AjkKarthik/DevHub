addMocksToSchema Defaults, Memoization and preserveResolvers

Strings are always Hello World, not random. Mocks replace your real resolvers unless you opt out, and the same mocked schema repeats its values. All verified against a real @graphql-tools/mock install.

Where this fits

Mocked schemas suit component and contract tests where the data shape matters more than real values. When a real resolver is what you are testing, use the unit-test pattern from the main page or keep resolvers with preserveResolvers.
