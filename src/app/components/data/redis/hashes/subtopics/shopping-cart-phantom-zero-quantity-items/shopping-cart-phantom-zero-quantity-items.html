The Shopping Cart Challenge Leaves Phantom Zero-Quantity Items

The main page's own Shopping Cart Challenge solution stores a quantity-0 field instead of removing it — a realistic UI action (decrementing an item to zero) leaves a phantom line item sitting in the cart.
