Direct Reply-To Lets RPC Skip Declaring a Reply Queue

The RPC example declares an exclusive reply queue per call. RabbitMQ can route replies through the amq.rabbitmq.reply-to pseudo-queue instead, with a no-ack, same-channel rule and a durability trade-off.

Where this fits

This adds a built-in alternative to the reply-queue handling in the RPC example on the Patterns page. Whichever reply mechanism you use, the requester still needs a timeout.
