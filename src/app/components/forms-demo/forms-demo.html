Template-Driven vs Reactive Forms

When to use which?

FeatureTemplate-DrivenReactive
SetupFormsModule + ngModelReactiveFormsModule + FormBuilder
ValidationHTML attributesValidators array — fully type-safe
TestingHarder (needs DOM)Easier (pure TS objects)
Dynamic fieldsTrickyFormArray — straightforward
Best forSimple, small formsComplex / data-heavy forms
