Writing Your Own ADRs

Status

Proposed → Accepted → (Superseded | Deprecated). A Superseded ADR links to the ADR that replaced it.

Context

The situation and forces that drove the decision. Be specific — "performance was slow" is weaker than "P99 latency exceeded 800ms under 100 concurrent users".

Decision

The chosen approach. One clear paragraph. Not a list of options — that goes in Alternatives. Record what was decided, not what was considered.

Consequences

Positive and negative outcomes. Include the trade-offs you accepted — this is the most valuable part for future readers.

Alternatives

What else was considered and why it was rejected. Without this, future engineers repeat the same evaluation.

Storage

Store ADRs as Markdown files in the repo under docs/adr/. They live alongside the code and evolve with it. Tools: adr-tools CLI, Architecture Decision Records GitHub template.
