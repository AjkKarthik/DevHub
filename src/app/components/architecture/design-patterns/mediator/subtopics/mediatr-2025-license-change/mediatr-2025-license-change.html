MediatR's 2025 Commercial License Change

The main page calls MediatR "the de facto .NET Mediator for CQRS" — still true in adoption terms, but since v13 (July 2025) the library ships under a dual license, free only under a $5M revenue threshold. Here's exactly what changed and what didn't.
